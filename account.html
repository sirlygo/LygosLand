What syncs

And what deliberately doesn't.

  • Profiles, marks and colourssynced
  • Playtime, favourites, recently playedsynced
  • Game files and BIOS filesstay on your device
  • Emulator save statesstay on your device

Game files aren't uploaded on purpose. It would mean hosting copyrighted files on a server, and it would cost money — this way the site stays free and your files stay yours. Use the ROM folder picker on the Play page to point each device at its own collection.
