Controls

Click the screen first so it takes your input.

Keyboard is mapped by default — arrows to move, Z and X for the face buttons, Enter for start. A USB or Bluetooth gamepad is picked up automatically once you press a button on it. Remap anything from the gear icon in the emulator's own toolbar.

About BIOS files

Why some systems ask for one.

Disc-based consoles won't start without the firmware that shipped inside the original hardware. This site doesn't hand those out — you supply your own, dumped from a console you own. Whatever you pick is kept in your browser's local storage, so it's a one-time step per system, and it never leaves your device. Forget wipes it.
